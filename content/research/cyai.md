## Abstract

Our research paper is motivated by the critical need for “bilingual” professionals fluent in both cybersecurity and AI professional competencies to navigate the high-powered frontier of CyAI. The study's research question is: *What cybersecurity and artificial intelligence (CyAI) convergent competencies are expected of today's computing professionals?* The research methodology used scoping review of grey literature sources and integrative review of identified competencies in order to synthesize CyAI convergent competencies. Grey literature (e.g., government workforce frameworks and industry reports) provides timely evidence not currently published in academic literature. The qualitative analysis of the professional competencies collected from selected sources resulted in 261 competencies: 67 cyber-independent, 105 AI-independent, 59 cyber applied to AI, 22 AI applied to cyber, and 8 CyAI convergent competencies. Competencies related to applying cyber to AI or applying AI to cyber were grouped into 15 *CY* → *AI* cluster competencies and 5 *AI* → *CY* cluster competencies. The iterative process of the researchers’ synthesis and verification of cluster competencies yielded the CyAI convergence. The main contributions of this study are: 1) the synthesis of professional cybersecurity and AI competencies into directionally coupled clusters and 2) an initial set of CyAI convergence competencies for critique. We discuss the findings’ implication for computing education and conclude with future directions for cross-discipline collaborations and academic-industry partnerships.
